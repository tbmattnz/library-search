# Fixture provenance

All holdings, catalogue IDs, shelves, copy counts, availability, newspaper and journal records are fictional. Publication years and authors for the two novels describe the works, not an actual library edition.

## Literary excerpts

- Lewis Carroll, _Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland_ (1865), Chapter I. [Source text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11/pg11-images.html). Three selected paragraphs. [Source catalogue](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11) identifies the work as public domain in the USA. No illustrations or modern editorial material are included.
- Jane Austen, _Pride and Prejudice_ (1813), Chapter I. [Source text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1342/pg1342-images.html). First two paragraphs, with the split typographic opening “I T” normalised to “It”. [Source catalogue](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342) identifies the work as public domain in the USA. No illustrations or modern editorial material are included.

Only the underlying literary passages are reproduced, without Project Gutenberg branding or ebook packaging. Source links are supplied for provenance. These are excerpts, not complete digitised books. Demo page numbers are deliberately assigned to this fixture and are not printed-edition page numbers.

## Original fixture text

The Example Gazette passages and all fictional catalogue/holdings data were created for this demonstration and are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They are not historical records.

There is no customer data, private commercial source, real library export or paid API in this repository.
